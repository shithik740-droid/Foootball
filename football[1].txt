FOOTBALL – BASIC INTRODUCTION

Football, also known as soccer in some countries, is a team sport played between two teams. 

The main objective of football is to score more goals than the opposing team.

A football match is played on a rectangular field with a goal at each end. 

Players use their feet to control, pass, and shoot the ball.

Players may also use other parts of their body, such as the head and chest, but outfield players generally cannot deliberately use their hands or arms.
 
The goalkeeper is allowed to use their hands within their own penalty area.

Football is usually played as a competitive team sport. 

Each team attempts to attack and score goals while defending its own goal. The team that scores more goals by the end of the match wins.

If both teams score the same number of goals, the match is a draw unless the competition rules require a winner.

Football is governed internationally by the International Football Association Board (IFAB), which maintains the Laws of the Game. 

FIFA is the international governing organization for football and organizes major international competitions such as the FIFA World Cup.
